Flip 54

Fifty-four cards. One workout. Never the same twice.

Flip 54 is an iPhone workout app that turns a standard 54-card deck into a randomized workout. Each suit is a body target, each card's value is your rep count. Flip a card, do the reps, flip again.

No accounts, no ads, no subscriptions, no tracking. Everything stays on your device.
