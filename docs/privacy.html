Privacy Policy

Flip 54 · Effective November 1, 2026

Flip 54 is built to work without collecting anything about you. This policy explains exactly what that means.

Short version

Flip 54 does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The app saves your settings (difficulty, equipment, sound and haptics preferences), your workout history, your streaks and lifetime stats, and any workout you have in progress. This information is stored only on your iPhone and, if you use it, your Apple Watch, in the app's own storage. When you use Flip 54 on Apple Watch, your settings and workouts pass directly between your iPhone and your watch; they never go through a server. None of it is sent to me or to any third party, and I cannot see it.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Flip 54 uses Apple Health (HealthKit). On Apple Watch it reads your heart rate and active calories to show them during a workout, and saves each finished workout to Health. On iPhone, Health access is what lets a workout you start there open on your Apple Watch. Flip 54 reads only heart rate and active energy, and writes only workouts.

Health data stays in Apple Health on your devices. Flip 54 never sends it anywhere, never uses it for advertising, and never shares or sells it. You can change or turn off Flip 54's access at any time in the Health app or in Settings.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet or any server.
  • It does not require an account or sign-in.
  • It does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or third-party SDKs.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone, and it uses Apple Health only as described above.

Sharing

If you choose to share a workout result, the app uses the standard iOS share sheet. What you share and where it goes is controlled by you and the app you choose. I never receive it.

Children

Flip 54 collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Your data and deleting it

Because all data lives on your device, you control it. Deleting the app removes its stored data from your iPhone and Apple Watch. Workouts already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app. If you back up your device with iCloud or your computer, the app's data may be part of that backup under Apple's own terms.

Health note

Flip 54 is a fitness timer and workout generator, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise routine.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
