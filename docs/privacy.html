Privacy Policy

Flip 54 · Effective October 17, 2026

Flip 54 is built to work without collecting anything about you. This policy explains exactly what that means.

Short version

Flip 54 does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The app saves your settings (difficulty, equipment, sound and haptics preferences), your workout history, your streaks and lifetime stats, and any workout you have in progress. This information is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. It is not sent to me or to any third party, and I cannot see it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet or any server.
  • It does not require an account or sign-in.
  • It does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or third-party SDKs.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone, or health data.

Sharing

If you choose to share a workout result, the app uses the standard iOS share sheet. What you share and where it goes is controlled by you and the app you choose. I never receive it.

Children

Flip 54 collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Your data and deleting it

Because all data lives on your device, you control it. Deleting the app removes its stored data from your iPhone. If you back up your device with iCloud or your computer, the app's data may be part of that backup under Apple's own terms.

Health note

Flip 54 is a fitness timer and workout generator, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise routine.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
